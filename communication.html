Computing for Communication

Overview

Through the development of the internet, computing has made global communication more accessible than ever; people from across the world can communicate and share knowledge almost instantly.

Impacts


  1. Academic research

    2. Scientists and academics can collaborate from all over the world on problems and breakthroughs that further humanity's understanding of the world

  2. International trade

    3. Nations can easily enter international markets, stimulating economic growth and global partnerships

  3. Community

    4. People can find community and start relationships with other people, regardless of location

  4. Global awareness

    5. social problems and news can easily be spread to other parts of the world, increasing awareness of issues and events faced by people across the world
